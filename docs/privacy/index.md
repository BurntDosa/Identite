# Identité Privacy Policy

Updated 25 October 2026.

Identité stores your IDs and bank cards so you can copy them. The developer cannot see any of it.

## What the app stores

The IDs, cards, names and notes you enter, and card colours you choose or take from a scan. They are stored on your devices and, when iCloud is on, synced through your private iCloud database. In iCloud, ID values (including an optional CVV), ID nicknames, notes and person names are stored as encrypted fields; record identifiers, ID types, which person an ID belongs to, and when records were created or updated are not. There is no Identité server.

Device backups may include the app's data. Their protection and retention follow your device and backup settings.

Card PINs are never stored. A CVV is optional and needs Face ID, Touch ID or your device password each time it is shown or copied.

## What the app does not collect

No analytics, advertising, tracking, crash reporting or third-party SDKs. Nothing is sent to the developer. ID values are never written to logs.

## Camera

The camera reads an ID or card only while you scan. Recognition runs on the device. Photos are not saved.

## Clipboard

Anything copied from an ID, including Copy or Cut while editing an ID or a person's name, stays on this device without Universal Clipboard. It clears after 60 seconds, or 30 seconds for a CVV, and when the app quits. On a Mac, if the app crashes or is force-quit, a copy stays until something else is copied. Clearing cannot undo a paste another app already received.

## Deleting your data

Deleting an ID removes it from the app, its reminders and, through iCloud, your other devices. Older device backups are not changed. To remove everything, delete the app and remove its data in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

## Children

Identité is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
